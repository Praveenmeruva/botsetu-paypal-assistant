**Title:** BotSetu × PayPal – Book and pay in Telugu & Hindi over chat (PayPal AI Hackathon)

**Description:**
BotSetu is a multilingual AI booking assistant for small businesses in India, such as clinics, coaching centres and salons. Customers chat in Telugu, Hindi, Tamil or English. The agent books a slot and gets paid through PayPal.
